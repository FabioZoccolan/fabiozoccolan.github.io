FABIO ZOCCOLAN / NUMERICAL ANALYSIS

About me.

I am a PhD student in Mathematics (Numerical Analysis) at the École Polytechnique Fédérale de Lausanne (EPFL), under the supervision of Prof. Fabio Nobile and Dr. Yoshihito Kazashi. I am scheduled to defend my PhD thesis in December 2026.

In 2025, I spent 11 months as a visiting PhD researcher in Prof. Youssef Marzouk’s UQ Group at the Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT), working on data assimilation.

In 2021, I obtained my Master’s degree in Mathematics from the University of Trieste, in collaboration with SISSA, where I completed my Master’s thesis under the supervision of Prof. Gianluigi Rozza and Dr. Maria Strazzullo.

Research.

01

NUMERICAL ANALYSIS

Numerical Methods for Stochastic Differential Equations

My research focuses on the development and mathematical analysis of numerical methods for stochastic differential equations. I am particularly interested in numerical discretization schemes for stochastic dynamical systems, their convergence and stability properties, and the development of computationally efficient approximations.

A particular focus of my work is the numerical analysis of dynamical low-rank approximations for stochastic differential equations, including the study of approximation errors, time discretization, and mean-square stability.

02

FILTERING & SMOOTHING

Data Assimilation

My research in data assimilation focuses on filtering and smoothing for stochastic dynamical systems, with particular emphasis on computationally efficient methods for high-dimensional problems. I investigate particle-based approaches and dynamical low-rank approximations for state estimation and uncertainty quantification.

03

LOW-DIMENSIONAL APPROXIMATIONS

Reduced-Order Modelling

My research interests encompass reduced-order modelling in a broad sense, with a focus on the development and analysis of efficient numerical methods for approximating high-dimensional and complex dynamical systems.

A central direction of my research concerns dynamical low-rank approximation (DLRA), particularly for stochastic differential equations. I am interested in the mathematical foundations of these approximations, their numerical discretization, and their application to problems involving stochastic dynamics and uncertainty quantification.

More generally, I am interested in reduced-basis methods and reduced-order modelling for time-dependent problems. My previous work includes the development and analysis of stabilized reduced-basis methods for parametrized advection-dominated optimal control problems with random inputs, investigating in particular the role of streamline upwind Petrov–Galerkin (SUPG) stabilization during the offline and online stages.
